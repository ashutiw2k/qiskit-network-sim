### 3. Ground Truth Circuit (build_swapping_circuit_ground_truth)
STATUS: ✓ CORRECT

Implements quantum process tomography by measuring in three bases:
- Z-basis: Direct measurement → detects X and Y errors
- X-basis: H rotation before/after → detects Z and Y errors
- Y-basis: HS rotation before, S†H† after → detects X and Z errors

The basis rotations at both source and sink are intentional - errors accumulate
in the rotated basis during swapping, making different Pauli errors detectable
when rotated back before measurement.

### 4. Pauli Error Rate Calculation (calculate_exact_pauli_rates)
STATUS: ✓ CORRECT

Correctly solves the linear system from three measurement bases:
  P(X) = (E_z + E_y - E_x) / 2
  P(Y) = (E_z + E_x - E_y) / 2
  P(Z) = (E_x + E_y - E_z) / 2

Where:
  E_z = error rate in Z-basis = P(X) + P(Y)
  E_x = error rate in X-basis = P(Z) + P(Y)
  E_y = error rate in Y-basis = P(X) + P(Z)

Properly handles:
- Normalization by total shots
- Cleanup of small negative values from statistical noise
- Returns all relevant metrics
